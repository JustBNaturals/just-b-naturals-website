A simple note about your information.

Orders

When you submit an order, Just B Naturals receives the contact information and order details needed to confirm availability, payment, pickup, or delivery. This information is used to complete and discuss your order.

Promotional emails

You are added to the promotional email list only when you actively choose to subscribe. You can unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time. Placing an order does not automatically subscribe you.
