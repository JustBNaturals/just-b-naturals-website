Breast Cancer Awareness Month

About 1 in 8 women in Canada will develop breast cancer during their lifetime. Taking a moment to learn about screening can help you decide what to discuss with your doctor.

In Quebec, the screening program is for women aged 50–74 covered by public health insurance, with mammograms every two years. Under 50 or over 74, speak with your doctor about screening based on your personal risk. After a breast cancer diagnosis, follow-up is personalized.
