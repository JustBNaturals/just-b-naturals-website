Private stock manager

Inventory

Set the stock and price, choose Preorder only when a product is curing, then press Save changes. A stock number of 0 automatically shows Out of stock. Stock can stay blank when you do not want a number shown. Prices are in Canadian dollars.

Three simple choices

Available means customers can order it now, unless stock is 0. Preorder shows the ready date and allows advance orders. Unavailable turns ordering off even when stock is entered. Out-of-stock and unavailable products show one clear “Notify me” button.
