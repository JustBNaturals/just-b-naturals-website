Brigitte Gariepy’s story

A simple idea, made with care.

Just B Natural grew from my desire to make everyday care feel simpler, more thoughtful, and more personal. I create every product in small batches with carefully chosen ingredients and hands-on attention. What began as a simple idea has grown into a collection of soaps, body care, and home essentials that fit naturally into real life. I make each batch myself here in Gatineau, Québec.

Before soapmaking, I spent over 25 years as a baker and cake decorator. That work shaped my love of craft, detail, and creating with my hands. I’ve always been artsy and a little bit crafty, and after retiring from the baking world, I found myself drawn back to the basics: making sourdough, brewing kombucha, reconnecting with simple, traditional skills. Soap came next, and one batch led to another until Just B Natural took shape.

Today, I choose to grow old naturally, to use real ingredients, and to keep life as honest and uncomplicated as possible. My products reflect that philosophy: made with intention, crafted by hand, and designed to support a life that values simplicity, authenticity, and the beauty of staying true to yourself.
