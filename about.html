The heart of the brand

Thoughtful products for simple, everyday rituals.

The focus is uncomplicated: carefully considered ingredients, small-batch attention and products that are pleasant and easy to use. From a bar of handmade soap to a calming roller oil, every item is meant to fit naturally into real life.

Because batches are made personally and quantities can change, each online order is treated as a request. Availability, pickup or local delivery, and payment are confirmed directly before anything is finalized.
