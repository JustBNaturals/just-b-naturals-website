The homepage loads `/api/instagram` near the viewport and checks again every five minutes while the section is visible, and when returning to the tab. Unchanged feeds do not redraw. Playing videos and keyboard focus are not interrupted. Images and actual reel video streams pass through same-origin media routes; credentials and Instagram access tokens are never stored in the repository or sent to shoppers. Upstream URLs are restricted to Behold/Instagram media hosts; post URLs must be actual Instagram links. JSON is cached for ten minutes, media responses for an hour. Instagram/Behold synchronization adds its own delay: new posts are automatic, not guaranteed instantaneous. No redeployment is needed for new posts.

Desktop mouse hover previews a reel. On touch devices, a reel starts muted and inline when at least 55% visible; only one plays at a time, with the current reel kept stable until it leaves the visible threshold. Offscreen/hidden videos pause. Videos download only when playback is requested, not on initial page load. Manual play/pause controls remain available, and a manual pause is respected until the tile leaves the screen. Reduced-motion and data-saving preferences disable automatic playback. Browser autoplay restrictions (including some phone battery-saving modes) retain the poster and play button rather than repeatedly retrying. Clicking the photo opens its actual Instagram post. Local brand photos remain a fallback if the feed is unavailable. Overlay captions are hidden; accessible link labels and bilingual controls remain.
